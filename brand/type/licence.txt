BAGARI — brand type
===================

The site is set in Futura 100 GEO, a Georgian cut of Futura. One family
covers Latin, Mkhedruli and Mtavruli, so the two scripts share a single
face rather than being paired across two families.

  Futura 100 GEO Text   — body copy, UI, labels   (400, 500, 600, 700)
  Futura 100 GEO        — large headings          (600, 700)

The .woff2 files here are subsets of the supplied OTFs, cut down to the
ranges the site actually uses: Basic Latin, Latin-1, general punctuation,
the lari sign, arrows, Mkhedruli (U+10A0–10FF) and Mtavruli (U+1C90–1CBF).
Roughly 48 KB each. The source OTFs are deliberately NOT in this repo —
see below. Erekle holds them locally; they are also in git history up to
commit 28596c8 if another weight is ever needed.
